Frequently asked questions

How does AutoSettle work?

PayFlow analyzes settlement routes in real-time, comparing NEFT, IMPS, and RTGS on cost, speed, and success rate. It automatically selects the optimal route and executes the transfer. Average savings: 2-5% per settlement.

Which processors do you support?

We currently support Razorpay, Stripe, and Wise. More processors coming soon. Our unified API normalizes data across all processors.

Is my payment data secure?

Yes. We use bank-grade encryption, are PCI DSS compliant, and never store card details. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Can I try before I buy?

Absolutely. Our Free tier gives you full access to the dashboard. The 14-day trial includes all automation features.

What's the ROI?

Most merchants see 2-5% cost savings on settlements and 25% more disputes won. That's typically 10x the subscription cost in month one.

How long does setup take?

5 minutes. Connect your processor API keys, enable autopilot, and PayFlow starts working immediately.
